Privacy Policy — Momentum

Effective Date: October 2026 · Developer: NeuroFlow Studio (Vinay Reddy)

1. Local-First Data Storage

Momentum is designed as a privacy-first executive function utility. All tasks, micro-steps, timer history, streaks, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not transmit, read, or store your personal task lists on external servers.

2. Audio & Permissions

All neural soundscapes (Brown Noise, Pink Noise, Green Noise, and Binaural frequencies) are synthesized locally in real time on your device using the Web Audio API. Momentum does not record microphone audio or access your camera, contacts, or location.

3. Purchases & External Links

In-app upgrades on Android are processed securely via Google Play Billing. Optional links to companion products on Amazon open in your external browser.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, clearing the app storage or uninstalling Momentum permanently deletes all stored tasks and progress immediately.
