Privacy Policy for Momentum ADHD Planner

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 · Developer: Vinay Reddy

This Privacy Policy explains how Momentum ADHD Focus Planner ("we", "our", or "the App") handles user information. We are firmly committed to user privacy and offline-first data ownership.

1. Summary: Zero Personal Data Collection

Momentum is designed as an offline-first executive functioning application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal tasks, routines, audio preferences, or brain dumps on any remote server. All your focus lists, streaks, and settings remain solely on your local device.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Task Data & Micro-Steps: Stored locally using device storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).
  • Dopamine Scores & Streaks: Calculated and maintained locally on your phone.
  • Audio Synthesizer State: Computed in real-time memory via the WebAudio API; no audio files are streamed from remote servers.

3. Third-Party Services & In-App Purchases

To support app maintenance and optional premium features, the App integrates with certified industry providers:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes premium subscriptions. All payment information (credit cards, UPI, billing addresses) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your financial details.
  • Google AdMob (Rewarded Video Ads): May display non-personalized rewarded video advertisements to users who opt to watch an ad in exchange for temporary Pro access. Google AdMob may process device identifiers and ad metrics according to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Security

Because your task data is stored locally on your device, deleting the App or clearing app storage will permanently remove your stored tasks.
